Institutional Research Analytics Platform

A production research-intelligence system that reconciles fragmented external evidence with institution-maintained records. It preserves every source row, resolves publication identity conservatively and enriches the research graph through governed APIs. A normalized source of truth feeds an analytical star schema, organizing measurable activity as facts and reporting context as dimensions for department, faculty, quality, collaboration and graduate-research intelligence.

Problem

Publication exports, external APIs and university operational records use different identifiers, update cycles and definitions.

Engineering

Separate raw capture from semantic normalization, protect internal data, then publish governed facts and dimensions for repeatable institutional reporting.

Outcome

Leadership dashboards use defensible CEN attribution, journal quality, collaboration and per-regular-faculty measures.

Scopus / SciValCrossrefOpenAlexPostgreSQLGoverned ETL / ELTEntity resolutionStar schema · facts + dimensionsSentence TransformersFastAPINext.jsHuman audit gate
Technical evidence

1 · Governed source capture

SciVal and Scopus Excel/CSV/TXT loaders detect real headers, fingerprint supported files against 148 governed provider headers and retain each original row as JSON plus source-named columns. File SHA-256, row hashes, import batches and ingestion runs establish replayable lineage.

2 · Conservative identity resolution

Scopus is the publication system of record. Missing EIDs resolve in an explicit confidence order: Scopus EID, Scopus-link reconstruction, SciVal DOI and unique exact title. Unresolved rows remain in raw storage; the pipeline never invents identifiers.

3 · Normalized research graph

Idempotent normalization creates publications, authors, authorships, journals, year-sensitive metrics, classifications, topics, affiliations, dates and cited-reference structures. This governed source of truth feeds an analytical star schema: publications, metrics, authorships, grants, patents and 2/N measures form the facts; author/faculty, journal, institution, department, date, topic and classification provide the dimensions. Crossref and OpenAlex responses are cached, with refresh policies controlling repeat enrichment.

4 · Protected institutional model

Faculty, instructors, departments, grants, patents/IP, research personnel and graduate-student records live in the separate internal schema. External ingestion cannot overwrite institution-maintained data; Scopus author IDs provide the reporting bridge.

5 · Reviewable ML matching

Graduate-publication matching uses exact Scopus IDs and deterministic name rules first, then Sentence Transformers when needed. Compatibility gates, confidence thresholds, persisted review states and advisor scope keep semantic similarity from becoming an unchecked match.

6 · Operational analytics

A protected FastAPI service and role-aware Next.js portal deliver publication browsing, faculty profiles, grants, patents, metrics and ingestion control. Dashboards cover Q1–Q4, top percentiles, collaboration, department contribution, 2/N coverage and output per regular faculty.

Four technical workflows

Drone imagery · Gemini + SAM 2

GeoTIFF / COG → crop, tile and reproject → Gemini bounding boxes → SAM 2 pixel masks → map coordinates → repaired building polygons.

Reference GIS

OSMnx or institutional polygons → geometry repair → CRS alignment → deduplication → area, centroid, overlap and building IDs.

Satellite change · Prithvi

STAC search → signed assets → Stackstac/Xarray → cloud mask → band differences, spectral indices or Prithvi embeddings → threshold → polygonized change layer.

Anomaly model suite

Engineered land metrics feed Isolation Forest for global anomalies, LOF for peer-group anomalies, HDBSCAN for unusual clusters, graph methods for coordinated relationships and time-series models for unusual registry alterations.

Dublin historical archives · HTR · cadastral extraction

Dublin 18th-Century Wide Streets Commission Transcription Project

An auditable pipeline built around locally deployed vision models for Dublin’s eighteenth-century commission records. It preserves the scan, page identity, regions, line crops, model decisions and structured cadastral fields as one evidence chain. Page preparation, orientation, classification and segmentation run through locally deployed models; external APIs sit behind an explicit adjudication boundary for unresolved handwriting or model disagreement.

64 archival pages1,682 segmented lines809 recognition crops90% local agreement
Model selection

Qwen2.5-VL-7B exhausted the A10G, while 8-bit compression reduced its accuracy. The locally deployed 3B model was both smaller and more accurate on this cursive.

Local witness design

Qwen2.5-VL-3B and 8-bit GLM-4.1V-9B provide independent semantic judgments; Surya and Kraken provide complementary region and line geometry.

Adjudication boundary

Claude, Gemini or GPT can be called for difficult text or disagreement. Unresolved words remain marked and route to needs_human_review.

Complete processing progression and model decisions

01 · Ingest: pdf2image assigns every page a permanent ID so evidence cannot be mixed downstream.

02 · Render: pdf2image and Pillow render fresh 300-DPI PNGs from the PDF, prioritizing chain of custody over shortcuts.

03 · Enhance: Pillow applies greyscale conversion and autocontrast locally while preserving the original page.

04 · Orientation and page hint: locally deployed Qwen2.5-VL-3B works with an OpenCV aspect-ratio check. Gemini 3.1 Pro is an escalation option. The 7B model exhausted the A10G and 8-bit compression hurt accuracy; 3B performed better on this cursive.

05 · Page-type classification: Qwen2.5-VL-3B and locally deployed GLM-4.1V-9B at 8-bit act as two independent witnesses. Gemini adjudicates only when they disagree.

06 · Region segmentation: Surya and Kraken draw geometry; Qwen and GLM assign region semantics. Gemini can fuse conflicting evidence as an optional adjudicator.

07 · Line segmentation: Surya and Kraken produce line geometry locally. Kraken reuses layout work from the previous phase; only disagreement needs escalation.

09 · Line crops: Pillow produces 809 single-line images—the input shape expected by handwriting recognizers.

10 · Transcription: TrOCR was rejected after failing on the cursive. Qwen-3B, GLM-9B, Kimi-VL and DeepSeek-OCR form the local-capable reader pool. The current difficult-cursive pass used Claude Sonnet 4.6, with Gemini 3.1 Pro as tie-breaker; GPT remains optional. Unresolved text stays marked [?] or [illegible] and routes to human review.

12 · Page assembly: pure Python restores line crops to page reading order without another model call.

13 · Cadastral extraction: parties, parcels and amounts enter a fixed schema, with every field traceable to its source line. The current extraction pass used Claude Sonnet 4.6; a local LLM can replace this boundary.

14 · Cross-registry validation — deferred: GeoPandas and fuzzy matching require a real reference land registry that is not currently available.

15 · Risk routing — deferred: the rule engine will route high-risk fields to humans after cross-registry validation exists.

16 · Report site: Python generates the auditable HTML deliverable and states completed, escalated and deferred work explicitly.

OxCOVID19 Database

Published in Scientific Reports, the OxCOVID19 Database created a unified international resource connecting epidemiology, government responses, mobility, weather, administrative divisions and national statistics. The project transformed more than 50 heterogeneous official sources into geographically and temporally comparable records for cross-country COVID-19 research.

50+ official sourcesEpidemiologyGovernment responseMobilityWeatherAdministrative geographyCountry statisticsInternational comparison
Paper scope and achievement
  • Combined epidemiological observations with policy measures, mobility, weather and demographic context in one research database.
  • Harmonized inconsistent reporting structures, dates and administrative geographies across more than 50 official data sources.
  • Supported comparable analysis across countries and subnational regions instead of leaving researchers to reconcile each source independently.
  • Created a reusable, extensible data foundation for studying transmission, interventions, mobility patterns and environmental conditions.
  • Published as an open, peer-reviewed data resource in Scientific Reports, a Nature Portfolio journal, in 2021.

NeuroTopo

An HPC pipeline processing roughly 286,000 reconstructed neurons into topological descriptors and a 286,000 × 286,000 distance matrix for morphology analysis.

PythonNumPy / SciPyHDF5SLURMNetworkX
Technical evidence

Eight Sholl step-function descriptors become 400 float32 features per neuron. Block-wise upper-triangular computation handles about 41 billion unique pairs; differential evolution learns descriptor weights over labeled classes.
